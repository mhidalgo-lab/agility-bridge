New tool `get_sprint` (planning group): one sprint with its UTC dates and schedule, backlog items and defects by status category, committed (at the begin date) vs done, scope change, carry-over of a finished sprint and a daily burndown read from bounded history. Sprint names and OIDs now also resolve closed (finished) sprints, and a status without a rollup category is no longer counted as "not started".
